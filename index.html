How ultimate works

Each square of the big board holds its own small game. Win a small game and that whole square becomes yours. Three squares in a row on the big board wins everything.

The square you play in decides where your opponent goes next: play top-right inside a small board and they must play in the top-right small board. The highlighted board shows where the current player has to go.

Sent to a board that is already won or full? You can play anywhere instead.
